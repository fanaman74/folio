# Design system

Folio is a quiet utility inspired by the alignment and hierarchy of a well-organized file workspace. The task leads the page; no marketing hero or invented proof. The user selected a light public interface and direct implementation. Impeccable's launcher and concept seed were attempted but unavailable; the references guide the manual implementation.

Warm off-white background, white work surface, deep forest text, muted sage accents and a saturated forest-green action color. Inter is self-hosted for body and display type. Use generous outer margins, compact queue rows, 14px surface corners, thin separators and distinct focus outlines. A responsive two-column workspace contains the upload/queue area and output settings, becoming a single column on mobile. Compact format chips use consistent Lucide icons. A typographic wordmark and a small geometric file-transform mark identify the product.

One authored entrance reveals the work surface; reduced motion disables it. All controls have visible focus, hover, busy, disabled and error states. Conversion progress reports actual completed files, and the active file is indeterminate. No fabricated conversion percentages.

## Intro page

The user later asked for an animated intro page in the spirit of getrubric.app while keeping this palette and type. It lives at `/`; the converter moved to `/convert`. The hero has a triangulated sage background that gently deforms and highlights nearby facets on mouse movement, then settles when the pointer leaves. It stops updating while offscreen or hidden; reduced motion keeps its geometry static with gentle colour feedback. Touch devices show the static mesh. Motion also includes the word-by-word blur-up headline, a looping mock conversion in a perspective-tilted preview that flattens on scroll, a format marquee, scroll-revealed cards with a cursor spotlight, count-up stats built from real service limits, and a slowly rotating gradient on the closing call to action. Reduced motion shows every element in its final state.

## Dark terminal look (November 2026)

The user asked for the site to match the visual style of osinto.io, while keeping Folio's own content, name and features. Both pages now default to a dark slate canvas (#0b0e13) with a neon green accent (#00ff88) and a light theme you can switch to from the header (the choice is saved in localStorage). Inter stays for body text; JetBrains Mono (self-hosted) is used for the wordmark, headings, labels and buttons, with labels in uppercase and wide letter spacing. Buttons are outlined and lightly tinted green, and they glow on hover. Surfaces are glassy gradient tiles with an inset hairline border and a deep drop shadow. Behind every page, four layered green hills drift slowly (`src/Waves.tsx`), with a faint dot grid in the top-left corner. The converter dims them so the work surface stays calm. The triangulated hero mesh was removed in favour of the hills. Colours live in `src/tokens.css` (dark on `:root`, light on `:root.light`), and the look-specific rules live in `src/theme.css`. Reduced motion stops the hills.
